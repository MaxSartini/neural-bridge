## Phase 7 closure decision

Phase 7 preserves three distinct steps:

1. The bounded diagnostic passed ranking/lift but failed the exact-value gates. This failure is necessary context.
2. Fresh blocked confirmation established the candidate outside discovery.
3. The final grouped held-out-video matrix passed all gates: `420/420` rows and `15/15` fold-groups versus frozen AR and matched controls on both primary metrics.

The defensible result is ranking and top-tail selection of future arousal movement, not exact decimal trajectory prediction. Final grouped Spearman was `0.2603011121` versus frozen AR `0.2405371348`; top-5% lift was `0.0975979581`, and the bridge-added top-5% margin over AR was `0.0080315818`. The phase also retained event ranking as secondary evidence.

The protocols are intentionally reported separately: blocked confirmation scored the residualized 4–10-row target, while grouped confirmation scored the positive-transformed raw 4–10-row future increase. The sealed PCA row manifests define grouped fold identity because scikit-learn fold numbering can vary by version.

Its minimum Git closure package will contain the canonical evidence page, diagnostic and grouped preregistrations/reports, compact summary/metrics/audit/manifest files, and the final diagnostic/grouped runners. Full fold matrices and model outputs remain in the registered external run directories.

## Phase 6 closure decision

Phase 6 keeps the failed tuning branch because it established the correct boundary: a promising single-seed Optuna result did not robustly improve the reference recipe under locked multi-seed confirmation. Trial 4 then failed both the fresh blocked gate and the three-checkpoint comparison; the within-seed fixed blend also failed. These are concluded negative results, not candidates.

The useful discovery was stabilization by averaging independently trained checkpoints. Applied to the already validated reference recipe, it passed fresh blocked control-complete confirmation and then grouped held-out-video confirmation. Final grouped PR-AUC was `0.2343675680` versus frozen AR `0.2180497906` and best matched control `0.2179716645`, positive in `15/15` fold-groups with failed gates `[]`. That prospectively confirmed stabilization lesson—not the rejected Optuna parameters—transitions into Phase 7.

## Phase 5/5.5 closure decision

Phase 5 first established that a deterministic learned bridge could transform weak raw cortical predictions into useful signal. The eval-mode correction must stay visible. On the original grouped spike target, the frozen-AR residual bridge reached PR-AUC `0.2383409298`, `+39.95%` over direct AR-plus-raw and `+38.85%` over the Phase 4 bridge. A separate grouped continuous lane passed ranking/lift at Spearman `0.2232222830`, but did not establish exact-value or blocked continuous forecasting.

The first washout-gap target redesign failed: its binary candidate was only `+0.0001547973` over AR and below the best control. Adding causal temporal context changed the result. `short_temporal_conv_residual` passed the 10-seed blocked confirmation at PR-AUC `0.2670735630` versus frozen AR `0.2602336231` and best control `0.2593369051`, then grouped compatibility at `0.2313831909` versus AR `0.2174953276` and best control `0.2174209937`, positive in `50/50` fold-seeds.

The original grouped artifact's raw-prevalence label-permutation gate was inapplicable because the residual null retained frozen AR. The corrected verdict was recomputed from existing artifacts with no retraining and passed. Both the original failed verdict and the corrected reasoning survive; stale wording does not outrank the correction. The final 420-row artifact is an integrity-preserving assembly of `70` blocked and `350` grouped rows, with zero reruns.

The Git closure selects all 14 current Phase 5 transition packages, 22 current runners/summarizers, the evidence ladder, the prospective 420-row plan, and one assembly contract test (`6 passed`). Two large fitted PCA row-index exports remain under the registered external derived collection. The older all-phase review and historical-ladder roots duplicate already selected phase evidence and stale source snapshots; they are retained untouched only until final cutover approval, then become deletion candidates rather than new-repository authorities.

## Phase 4 closure decision

Phase 4 asked whether fold-safe compression and causal temporal aggregation could turn the dense cortical representation into a useful bridge. On the original grouped spike target, the promoted `AR_plus_PCA_plus_temporal_diagnostics` lane using `temporal_mean_2s_then_pca256` reached PR-AUC `0.1716477402`, above AR `0.1472512409` and direct AR-plus-raw `0.1702993599`. Grouped-video was the promotion protocol; blocked temporal evidence remained diagnostic.

The phase matters as the first controlled bridge, but its widths and windows are historical outcomes, not inherited truth for VEATIC 2.1. Its mixed 24.37 GB root has therefore been separated into fold-safe cache, PCA features, PCA components, and an 81 MB benchmark core. The benchmark core links those derived collections so the original runtime path still resolves. Phase 5 follows because fixed linear/PCA lanes left meaningful head capacity and residual-learning questions open.

The Git closure retains one authoritative manifest/promotion/metric/audit view and the three phase-owned scripts. It omits a duplicate metric view and the 33 MB fold-detail table because both remain in the registered external benchmark core. The existing contract test imports the old shared benchmark/service stack and is deferred to coherent live-source migration rather than recreating that monolith inside the study.

## Phase 3 closure decision

Phase 3 established the problem rather than a universal failure slogan. On the grouped spike target, raw cortical scored PR-AUC `0.1365791944` versus AR `0.1472512410`; direct AR-plus-raw improved to `0.1702993599`. On the short-delta target, raw (`0.1326428221`) and AR-plus-raw (`0.2018566911`) both remained below AR (`0.2084069413`). Raw did beat AR on the absolute-delta target (`0.1265399499` versus `0.1181862917`), so the evidence is target-specific.

The key lesson is that a 20,484-vertex representation reduced to fixed label-free block summaries was inconsistent and often weaker than persistence. Shuffled, random, timestamp, and quality/motion/luma controls remain part of the record. Phase 4 follows because fold-safe compression and causal temporal aggregation offered a principled way to retain useful structure without treating the raw tensor as a ready-made predictor.

The Git closure retains both preliminary and final compact runs, the final report, representation metadata, and the phase entrypoint. Dense features and raw matrices remain external. The shared benchmark implementation is deferred to coherent live-source migration rather than duplicated behind the small entrypoint.

## Phase 2 closure decision

Phase 2 established the strong target-specific persistence floor used by later comparisons. The final run used current and lagged arousal/history features, train-split target thresholds, train-only inner validation for ridge alpha, and kept blocked-temporal and grouped-video protocols distinct. Final blocked PR-AUC was `0.2036` for the spike target, `0.2619` for short delta, and `0.1160` for absolute delta; grouped PR-AUC was `0.1472512410`, `0.2084069413`, and `0.1181862917`, respectively.

Four earlier revisions produced materially different grouped spike scores and survive as development evidence, not interchangeable baselines. The final run is the canonical Phase 2 reference. Phase 3 follows by asking whether aligned raw cortical predictions add value over that learned persistence baseline.

The Git closure mirrors that authority boundary: four compact development directories, one final directory, the final report, and the phase entrypoint. The shared benchmark implementation remains pending coherent live-source migration.

## Phase 1 closure decision

Phase 1 proves the supervised table is truly aligned at 2 Hz using saved dense-cache timestamps; it does not collapse to 1 Hz or claim predictive signal. It contains `243575` dense rows, `243441` labeled rows, and all `995` videos retain labels. The `134` unmatched rows across `38` videos remain explicit, as do `4153` rows without sufficient AR history.

Continuous future-movement values and masks are stored in the aligned table. Binary q90 thresholds are selected inside each training fold, never from test labels. The label parquet, summary, and contract have been moved out of the hardware-named cache into their scientific phase, while old consumer paths continue through symlinks. Phase 2 follows because alignment alone does not establish whether recent arousal can predict the targets.

The Git closure retains the compact contract, summary, report, and alignment entrypoint. It does not duplicate the row-level parquet or fitted target state.

## Phase 0 closure decision

## Original VEATIC closure decision

Original VEATIC remains the historical discovery layer, not an obsolete embarrassment and not a recipe source for VEATIC 2.1. Its final 124-video v2 result supports future arousal event/spike ranking from mostly video-driven predicted cortical responses. The strongest blocked full-frame spike row used `cortical_pca64_delta`: PR-AUC `0.2536` versus AR `0.1969`, shuffled `0.1840`, and random `0.1944`. Balanced event-vs-stable PR-AUC was `0.3394`, `+0.0609` over AR. Causal temporal context improved all four focused rows, with `causal_past_2s` strongest.

The failure boundary is equally important: continuous future-change remained diagnostic, and zero-change baselines beat the cortical features in most exact-value checks. Offset scans did not justify a global lag correction. The final evidence is mostly visual (`122/124` video-only cache entries), not full multimodal TRIBE. The post-v2 raw-representation tensors and trained-head audits remain historical evidence only.

Heavy assets now have scientific roles: canonical videos/ratings, feature caches, model-ready tensor export, post-v2 audit runs, temporal studies, and the protected evidence snapshot. The former benchmark root was a mixed operational directory, not one frozen study: it is now a compatibility-only view over those collections, while its later V-JEPA 2.1 encoder experiments belong to the VEATIC 2.1 foundation. A download archive and its seven-part copy were proven byte-identical; both remain quarantined until deletion approval. The external legacy alias was also confirmed to resolve to the same physical external root, so it is not a second evidence store.

The lightweight reconciliation compared the current and earlier benchmark trees by hash. The current 55-file closure is retained intact; the earlier tree's superseded lag-repair and 89-video device-consistency artifacts are not promoted. The Git study adds only 12 core/final runners and the self-contained strict scientific contract test (`3 passed`). Application-coupled test variants remain with shared live code until that code is migrated. Tracked source files stay untouched until final repository cutover, with source and destination hashes recorded.

## Naming rule

Destination names describe programme, scientific phase, and artifact role. Hardware, provider, machine, drive, archive, and temporary bundle names belong only in provenance. Material found inside such a bundle is renamed and placed under its actual scientific owner; the bundle name never becomes project taxonomy.
